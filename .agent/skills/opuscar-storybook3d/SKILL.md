# Lemo-Opuscar No.38: Storybook 3D Alpine Aerial Skill

## Core Concept
Adapted from Lemomo AI's `lemo-opuscar` (Opuscar 98, Entry #38 - *The Sound of Music*, 1965).
Delivers a signature storybook 3D aesthetic: sculpted rounded geometry, vibrant 1960s widescreen saturation, and dynamic cinematic aerial-to-orbit camera movement.

## Color Palette & Visual DNA
- **Grass**: Saturated Emerald (`#3FA33A`)
- **Sky**: Deep Cobalt (`#2F6FD0`)
- **Peaks & Clouds**: Clean White, cotton-puff cumulus
- **Atmospheric Haze**: 25% Lilac on farthest mountain ridges only
- **Rock Strata**: Warm grey-ochre layered bands
- **Lighting**: Bright high sun slightly behind camera, soft cloud shadows sweeping ground

## Camera Choreography (Continuous 3-Phase Shot)
1. **Phase 1 (0-2s) - High Gliding Aerial**: High-altitude pass cutting through cotton-ball cumulus over sculpted snow-capped peaks.
2. **Phase 2 (2-4s) - Hilltop Descent**: Rapid swooping descent toward the meadow, subject centered small in frame amidst patchwork fields.
3. **Phase 3 (4s+) - Low-Angle Grass Orbit**: Camera drops into knee-high grass and wildflowers, orbiting around the spinning subject at ~20°/s. Subject occupies ~1/4 frame height.

## AI Video / Image Prompt Formula
```text
Storybook 3D landscape in saturated 1960s widescreen colour: smooth sculpted forms, not low-poly or photoreal. Bright high sun behind camera: emerald grass #3FA33A, cobalt sky #2F6FD0, clean white snow and clouds. Rounded ridges, snow lines following slope, grey-ochre rock bands, cloud shadows. Farthest ridges with 25% lilac atmospheric haze. Cumulus as lit white cotton puffs. Knee-high grass and wildflowers fill foreground, bending in gusts. Continuous camera movement: high flight descent into a low orbit through the grass around the subject. Cinematography inspired by Ted McCord (The Sound of Music, 1965) and Hayao Miyazaki's lush meadows.
Negative Prompt: photoreal rock, sharp needle peaks, violet color cast, static grass, low poly faceted polygons, modern artifacts, blur.
```
